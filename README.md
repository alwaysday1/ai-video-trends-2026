## How these videos are made

Almost every trend here is a two-step pipeline:

1. **Photo to still.** An image model puts the person (or pet) from one photo into the trend's scene: the outfit, the setting, the light. Getting the still right first is cheaper than regenerating video.
2. **Still to video.** An image-to-video model animates the still: the motion, the camera move, and often generated sound. Clips are vertical (9:16) and short, usually 6 to 10 seconds.

Couple trends add one step at the start: two photos, taken apart, become one still of both people before the video step.

A prompt pattern that works for most of them:

```
Still:  [the person from the photo], [outfit], [setting], [light], vertical 9:16 portrait, face clearly visible
Video:  [one main action], [camera move], [mood], [sound], 8 to 10 seconds, no text on screen
```

Tips that apply to every trend:

- Start from a sharp, front-facing photo in daylight. A blurry photo is the most common reason the result does not look like the person.
- Keep the video prompt to one main action. Two or three actions in ten seconds usually blur together.
- Describe sound explicitly if the model generates audio; otherwise you get generic music.

## Rules we follow

- Adults only. No photos of anyone under 18, in any trend.
- Use your own photo, or a photo of someone who agreed to it. Both people in a couple trend should agree.
- No celebrities, real public figures or brand logos in prompts.
- Label the result as AI-generated where the platform asks you to.
